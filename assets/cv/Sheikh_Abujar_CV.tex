\section{Summary}

\small
\justifying

Applied AI researcher and Ph.D. Candidate in Computer Science at the
University of Alabama at Birmingham (UAB), developing machine learning
methods for complex scientific and real-world systems. Research spans
multivariate time-series forecasting, multimodal learning, transformer
architectures, large language and vision-language models, longitudinal
trajectory modeling, and computer vision. Experience applying AI across
heterogeneous domains including remote sensing and environmental monitoring,
healthcare, natural language processing, and large-scale consumer AI.
Developed and evaluated deep learning systems for temporal dynamics,
multimodal data integration, predictive modeling, and representation
learning. Previously worked as a Machine Learning Research Intern with the Bixby ML team at Samsung Research America, contributing to large-scale machine learning pipelines and production-oriented AI workflows. Author and co-author of peer-reviewed
AI/ML research with 1,380+ Google Scholar citations.
