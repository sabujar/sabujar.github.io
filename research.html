Research

I develop machine learning methods for systems that evolve over time — where signals are heterogeneous, multimodal, and observed across different scales and resolutions. My aim is to build models that are both principled and practical: lightweight enough to deploy, yet expressive enough to capture the temporal and cross-variable structure that real scientific and clinical systems exhibit.

Selected Projects

Time Series · Adaptive Modeling · PyTorch

STAR: Spectral and Trend Adaptive Regime-Guided ForecastingBest Paper

A lightweight multivariate forecasting architecture that adaptively routes signals using spectral, trend, and cross-variable characteristics, capturing heterogeneous temporal regimes for long-horizon prediction. Evaluated on ETTh1/2, ETTm1/2, and Exchange across horizons of 96–720. Best Student Paper Award, IEEE IRI 2026.

Time Series · Counterfactual Reasoning

Counterfactual Utility Routing & Cross-Variable Dynamics

Ongoing work on learning which forecasts to trust through counterfactual utility routing, and on recovering directed cross-variable dynamics via counterfactual interventions (RIVET). Two manuscripts under review, 2026.

Remote Sensing · Multimodal Deep Learning

Multimodal Algal Bloom Severity Modeling

A multimodal deep learning approach for detecting and evaluating harmful algal bloom severity by integrating satellite imagery, climate variables, and elevation data. Published at the IEEE ICME Workshops (ICMEW).

LLMs · Retrieval-Augmented Generation

Chronofy: Time-Aware Retrieval-Augmented Generation

A temporal reasoning framework for RAG that incorporates information validity and temporal relevance into retrieval and reasoning, reducing temporal hallucination from plausible but outdated retrieved content. IEEE IRI 2026.

Clinical AI · Longitudinal Modeling

Longitudinal Prediction of BK Virus-Associated Nephropathy

Machine learning frameworks that model kidney-transplant patient trajectories from longitudinal clinical measurements and heterogeneous patient data, developed in collaboration with physicians and biomedical researchers.

Computer Vision · Medical Imaging

Medical Image & Vision Modeling

Medical-image pipelines incorporating DICOM preprocessing, region extraction, OCR-based label recognition, and patch-level spatial analysis.
