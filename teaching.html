Teaching & Experience

Teaching Philosophy

My teaching rests on two convictions: that education is fundamentally about human development, and that a student's time is their most valuable resource. Inspired by Randy Pausch's The Last Lecture — “the key question to keep asking is, are you spending your time on the right things?” — I design courses that help students focus their effort on the ideas and skills that compound over a career.

I also see teaching and research as inseparable. The idea that shaped my decision to become an educator — “in learning, you will teach, and in teaching, you will learn” — still guides how I run a classroom. In practice, I pair rigorous fundamentals with hands-on projects, encourage students to learn by building, and mentor them toward independent inquiry.
