I am a Ph.D. candidate in Computer Science at the University of Alabama at Birmingham, developing machine learning methods for complex temporal and scientific systems. My research spans multivariate time-series forecasting, multimodal learning, and transformer architectures, with applications across remote sensing, healthcare, and large-scale AI.

I build models that reason about how systems evolve over time — where signals arrive at different scales, resolutions, and modalities. Recent work includes STAR, a spectral- and trend-adaptive architecture for long-horizon forecasting that received the Best Student Paper Award at IEEE IRI 2026. I have also worked on multimodal environmental modeling, time-aware retrieval-augmented generation, and longitudinal clinical prediction, and I previously interned with the Bixby ML team at Samsung Research America.

I am on the academic job market and seeking tenure-track assistant professor positions beginning Fall 2027.
